## About Me

Hi, I'm **Livio**. I live in the canton of Zug in Switzerland, and I'm doing an apprenticeship as a **platform developer (Informatik EFZ)** at **Convotis Schweiz AG** in Rotkreuz. I go to vocational school at the **TBZ (Technische Berufsschule Zürich)**.

### What I do at work

At Convotis I set up notebooks for our customers, and I'm often the person who takes care of the IT for these devices. I also help with internal projects, for example moving our old OTRS templates into our new Jira system.

### My hobbies

- **Ice hockey:** I'm involved in refereeing for the Swiss Ice Hockey Federation (SIHF). I help with the supervision of referees and with assigning referees to youth games (U9 to U16).
- **Fantasy football:** I'm the commissioner of an NFL fantasy football league on Sleeper. My own team is called *"Safety first"*.
- **Coding projects:** I built my own offline web app that helps with referee supervision. It runs on GitHub Pages and can export reports as JSON and PDF.
