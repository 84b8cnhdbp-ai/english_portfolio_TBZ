> **Situation:** I'm a second-year apprentice and I'm about to move to another department. My superior asked me to create a booklet for the apprentice who will take over my job. Here I collect ideas about my tasks and what could go into this learning documentation.

## Background info

### What's the name of your company?

Convotis Schweiz AG in Rotkreuz. Convotis is an IT service provider.

### What tasks do you carry out on a regular basis?

- Setting up new notebooks for customers (installation, updates, software, user accounts)
- Handling support tickets
- Moving our old OTRS templates into our new Jira system
- Small office tasks, for example ordering snacks for the team
- Researching and comparing products, for example water dispensers with a fixed water connection for the office

### Which of these is a complex task?

**Setting up a notebook for a customer.** It has many steps, every customer has different requirements, and at the end the device must work perfectly when the customer receives it.

### Why do you like or dislike doing this task?

I like it because I can work independently and I see a finished result at the end. I also learn a lot about operating systems, software deployment and security. What I don't like so much is when I have to wait for long updates or when information from the customer is missing.

### How do you manage this complex task?

- I use a **checklist** for every device, so I don't forget a step.
- I check the customer's requirements in the **ticket** before I start.
- I write down the **serial number** and the important settings.
- I **test** the notebook at the end (login, network, printer, software).
- When I'm not sure, I ask a colleague before I continue.

### How can you organise clear and effective explanations of a complex task?

- Split the task into **small, numbered steps**.
- Use **screenshots** for every important screen.
- Explain **why** a step is important, not only **how** to do it.
- Add a **"common problems"** section with solutions.
- Use simple language and explain technical terms in a glossary.

### What are the dos and don'ts with regard to this task?

| Dos | Don'ts |
|---|---|
| Follow the checklist step by step | Skip steps because you're in a hurry |
| Install all updates before handover | Hand over a device without testing it |
| Document serial numbers and settings | Write passwords on sticky notes |
| Ask when the requirements are unclear | Guess what the customer wants |
| Encrypt the hard drive (e.g. BitLocker) | Use your own account on a customer device |

### What issues would you have to deal with if you forgot an essential step?

- If I forget **encryption**, the customer's data isn't protected if the notebook is lost or stolen.
- If I forget **updates**, the device can have security problems, and the customer loses time on the first day.
- If I forget to **join the device to the domain / device management**, the customer can't log in or get their software.
- If I forget to **document** the device, nobody knows later who has which notebook.

In the end, the customer is unhappy, and I have to fix the problem, which takes much more time than doing it right the first time.

### What skills do you need to complete these tasks?

- Technical skills: Windows/macOS, networks, user accounts, software deployment
- Being careful and precise
- Time management (several devices at the same time)
- Communication skills (with customers and colleagues)
- Problem solving and patience
