> **Task:** Finish the sentence halves from page 41 in the textbook and say if each one is an example of **conditional 0** (general rule / always true) or **conditional 1** (specific situation / imperative).

| # | Sentence | Type |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Don't buy a second-hand car **unless a mechanic has checked it first.** | **1** – imperative, specific situation |
| 2 | You shouldn't think of getting a tattoo **if you aren't sure that you'll still like it in twenty years.** | **0** – general advice, always true |
| 3 | Keep a first-aid kit in your house **in case someone gets hurt.** | **1** – imperative |
| 4 | Children shouldn't use social media **until they are old enough to understand the risks.** | **0** – general rule |
| 5 | Always take out travel insurance **in case you get sick or your flight is cancelled.** | **1** – imperative |
| 6 | As soon as you've received your new credit card, **sign it and activate it in your banking app.** | **1** – imperative, specific situation |
| 7 | Don't go walking in the mountains on your own **unless you tell someone where you're going.** | **1** – imperative |
| 8 | If you are taking a new job abroad, **you will need to find out if you need a work permit.** | **1** – specific situation, *will* in the main clause |

## Grammar notes

- **Conditional 0:** *If / when + present simple, present simple.* It describes general rules and facts that are always true.
  *If you heat ice, it melts.*
- **Conditional 1:** *If + present simple, will / imperative / modal.* It describes a specific, possible situation in the future, or gives an instruction.
  *If it rains tomorrow, I'll take the train.* · *If you see Tom, tell him to call me.*
- **unless** = *if … not*: *Don't go unless you tell someone.* = *Don't go if you don't tell someone.*
- **in case** = as a precaution, because something *might* happen: *Take an umbrella in case it rains.*
- **until / as soon as** are time words. After them we use the **present** (or present perfect), not *will*:
  *As soon as you've received your card, …* (not: ~~as soon as you will receive~~)
