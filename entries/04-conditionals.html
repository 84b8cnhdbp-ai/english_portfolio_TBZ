Grammar notes

The rules behind it

0 Zero conditional

If / when + present, present

General rules and facts that are always true.

If you heat ice, it melts.

1 First conditional

If + present, will / imperative / modal

A specific, possible situation in the future, or an instruction.

If it rains tomorrow, I'll take the train.
If you see Tom, tell him to call me.

unless

unless = if … not

Don't go unless you tell someone. = Don't go if you don't tell someone.

in case · until · as soon as

+ present (not will)

in case = as a precaution: Take an umbrella in case it rains.

As soon as you've received your card, … – not as soon as you will receive
