> **Task:** Write environmental predictions using the **future perfect** (*will have + past participle*) and the **future continuous** (*will be + -ing*). Then write my thoughts on water consumption by AI.

**Grammar reminder**

- **Future perfect** – something will be finished before a point in the future: *By 2040, we **will have stopped** using plastic bags.*
- **Future continuous** – something will be in progress at a point in the future: *In 2040, people **will be driving** electric cars.*

## Predictions

### Fashion

- By 2040, most big brands **will have stopped** producing cheap fast fashion.
- In twenty years, people **will be renting** clothes for special events instead of buying them.

### Health and medicine

- By 2050, doctors **will have found** new ways to treat illnesses caused by air pollution.
- Hospitals **will be using** less single-use plastic, and they **will be recycling** more medical materials.

### Housing

- By 2045, Switzerland **will have replaced** most oil and gas heating systems with heat pumps.
- In the future, many people **will be living** in smaller flats with solar panels on the roof and green walls.

### Politics

- By 2035, most European countries **will have introduced** stricter laws against greenwashing.
- In ten years, young people **will be voting** more often for parties that focus on climate protection.

### Shopping

- By 2040, supermarkets **will have banned** most plastic packaging.
- People **will be buying** more local and seasonal food, and they **will be using** apps to check the CO₂ footprint of products.

### Social media

- By 2035, influencers **will have stopped** promoting extreme consumption, because their followers won't accept it anymore.
- In the future, social media platforms **will be showing** the environmental impact of the products in their ads.

## My thoughts: Water consumption via AI

AI needs big data centres, and these data centres use a lot of electricity. Many of them also use water to cool their servers, so every time we use an AI tool, some water is used in the background. Most people don't know this, because they only see the chat window on their screen.

In my opinion, this is a **real concern, but not the biggest one**. The problem depends a lot on *where* the data centres are. If a data centre is in a region where there isn't much water, it can compete with farmers and with the people who live there. In a country with a lot of water, like Switzerland, the problem is smaller. The source of the electricity is also important: AI powered by solar or hydro power is much better than AI powered by coal.

Should we use less AI? I don't think we have to stop using it, but we should **use it in a smart way**. For example, it doesn't make sense to ask an AI a simple question that a normal search or a calculator can answer. On the other hand, AI can also help the environment, for example by planning energy use better or by finding water leaks in pipes.

I think the **companies** have the biggest responsibility. They should build data centres in places with enough water and renewable energy, use better cooling systems, and publish how much water and electricity their AI uses. Then users can make better decisions too.
